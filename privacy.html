Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PDF Toolkit. We take your privacy seriously and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when using our mobile application.

Important: All your documents are processed locally on your device. We do not upload or store your PDF files on our servers.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Files and Documents: The app accesses files and photos you select for processing (scanning, compression, adding signatures). All files are processed locally.
  • Signatures: Electronic signatures you create are stored locally on your device.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: Anonymous statistics of app usage to improve functionality
  • Technical Data: Device model, operating system version, app version
  • Crash Data: Crash reports to improve app stability

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide core app functionality (scanning, compressing, signing PDFs)
  • Improve app performance and functionality
  • Analyze app usage to optimize user experience
  • Provide technical support to users
  • Fix bugs and improve stability

4. App Permissions

Our app requests the following permissions:

  • Camera: To scan documents and create PDFs from photos
  • File Access: To open, save, and export PDF documents
  • Storage: To save processed documents on your device
Note: You can revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings, however this may limit app functionality.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take all necessary measures to protect your data:

  • All documents are processed locally on your device
  • We do not upload your PDF files to external servers
  • Modern encryption methods are used to protect data
  • Access to the app is protected by built-in device security mechanisms

6. Data Sharing with Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal data to third parties, except:

  • Analytics Services: Anonymous usage data may be shared with analytics services to improve the app
  • Legal Requirements: When necessary to comply with legal obligations

7. Subscriptions and Payments

If you use paid features of the app:

  • Payment processing is handled through App Store / Google Play
  • We do not have access to your credit card information
  • Subscription information is stored in accordance with Apple and Google policies

8. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data from our system
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Restrict the processing of your data

9. Children's Data

Our app is not intended for children under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and discover that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

10. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this privacy policy. We will notify you of significant changes through:

  • In-app notifications
  • Updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this document

We recommend reviewing this page periodically for the latest information about our privacy practices.

11. International Data Transfer

Since all data is processed locally on your device, international data transfer is minimal and limited to anonymous analytics, which may be transmitted to servers in other countries in compliance with all applicable data protection laws.

12. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy or our data handling practices, please contact us:

  • Email: e@michil.ru
  • Feedback Form: Available in the app under "Support"

13. Consent

By using our app, you agree to the terms of this privacy policy. If you do not agree with any provisions of this policy, please do not use the app.

Your privacy is important to us. We are constantly working to improve the protection of your data and the transparency of our practices.
